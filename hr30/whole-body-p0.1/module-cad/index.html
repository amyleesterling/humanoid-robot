What these exports mean

The fabrication STEP for each module contains its current frame and removable-cover candidates. The integration-reference STEP adds the module-owned joint, hand, shell and installed-equipment geometry. Neither is a released part drawing: fasteners, bearing fits, tolerances, materials, harnesses, DFM, FAI, structural proof and physical validation remain open.
