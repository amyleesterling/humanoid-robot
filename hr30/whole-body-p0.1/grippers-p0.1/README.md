# HR-30 detailed bilateral grippers P0.1

**PRELIMINARY - CONFIGURATION AND PACKAGING CAD ONLY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

Two editable rack-and-pinion parallel-gripper assemblies replace the prior envelope-only hand refinement path. CLOSED and OPEN native STEP/GLB states, installed whole-robot views, individual candidate-part STEP files, kinematic/force/interface registers and a candidate BOM are included. Exact SHA-bound actuator geometry is retained in the native hand STEP assemblies and source register; the GLB and installed whole-robot views use a dimension-matched lightweight actuator body for practical web delivery. Exact tooth geometry, fits, materials, actuator interfaces, calibration, sensing, pinch safety, endurance, DFM, FAI and physical proof remain open.
