What changed

Direct 4S LiPo rejected

Its 14.8 V nominal voltage equals the XH/XM published maximum. The design no longer treats it as a direct actuator source.

TTL rails move to 9 V

Each two-axis XC330 branch gets its own regulator candidate instead of operating at the 12.0 V maximum.

Reset is not run

Manual reset can only restore safety-output eligibility. A separate deterministic motion command is always required.

Peak remains open

727 W equals 60.58 A at 12 V. Neither source candidate closes it, so torque/current limits are mandatory development inputs.

Two configurations, one boundary

  • Tether-first: external enclosed supply, safety relay and two series contactors; robot-side PDU and regulated branch rails.
  • Onboard later: 12 V 9 Ah LiFePO4 evaluation candidate only after tether load data, cassette redesign, fault current, charge and interruption evidence close.
