# HR-30 individual manufacturing files P0.1

**PRELIMINARY - CONFIGURATION AND PACKAGING CAD ONLY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

All 98 physical fabrication candidates have individual native STEP and SVG drawing-view exports. The 45 planar 2.5D candidates also have largest-face DXF profiles; the 24 removable covers have 0.15 mm / 0.15 rad STL meshes. Twelve nonmaterial harness corridors are intentionally excluded.

These files support design refinement and supplier discussion only. They are not released drawings or fabrication authority. Exact materials, stock, tolerances/GD&T, threads/inserts, print settings, DFM, FAI, capacity and physical validation remain open.
