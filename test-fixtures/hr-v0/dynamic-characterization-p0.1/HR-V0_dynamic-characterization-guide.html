The key boundary

DYNAMIXEL registers are useful corroboration, but USB and bus polling have no released host-latency or cross-channel timing guarantee. Primary stop-time and impact evidence therefore comes from one hardware-timed acquisition chain plus independent angle, current, voltage, force, displacement and trigger measurements.

No powered test in this guide is authorized. Existing E2-E4 gates and configuration-specific written approval still control execution.

DAQ candidate, not a selection

A LabJack T7 is a documented evaluation candidate because its stream is hardware-timed and its official table gives 12.5 kscans/s for eight +/-10 V addresses at resolution index 1. The exact scan list, ranges, isolation, signal conditioning, calibration, grounding, sample rate and timing budget remain SELECTION REQUIRED.

What this unlocks

Once built, calibrated, authorized and executed, this method can supply the missing physical inputs for moving mass and inertia, bumper/contact force, source-removal persistence, stopping time and guard proof-energy allocation. This file itself closes none of those tests.
