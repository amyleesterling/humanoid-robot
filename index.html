What exists now

Physical architecture

A recognizable head, articulated neck, torso, pelvis, two complete arms, two detailed rack-and-pinion grippers, two six-axis legs, ankles and feet, partitioned into 98 fabrication candidates.

Floating-base simulation

URDF and MJCF implement the same 25-axis schedule without anchoring the pelvis to the world, using provisional geometry, limits, mass and inertia.

Embodied-agent boundary

OpenAI may produce expiring structured behavior requests. Deterministic local software and independent hardware retain motion and permit decisions.

Native whole-body electrical

Nineteen KiCad sheets cover all 25 separately fed actuators, head HMI, pelvis IMU, eight foot sensors, tether-first energy and the isolated onboard-later path. ERC is 0 errors / 0 warnings for encoded connectivity only.

Eight simultaneous interfaces

Two routed six-layer four-channel carrier candidates implement five isolated RS-485 and three translated TTL channels. Independent layout, EMC, thermal and physical validation remain open.

Walking development

Suspended characterization, restrained standing, weight transfer, capture steps and tethered walking remain separate physical gates.
