## Disposition

V2.1 is a corrected, connected-ECAD preliminary package suitable for submission to qualified electrical and functional-safety reviewers. KiCad parses the root and all 14 child sheets, exports a 15-page A3 set and synchronized SVGs, produces a native netlist with 142 components and 168 named nets, and reports 0 ERC errors and 0 warnings.

That result is not approval. The block-symbol pins are deliberately passive where the physical device, connector, terminal, protection, conductor, or package is unresolved. Clean ERC proves only that the modeled names and connections are internally consistent. It does not prove physical pinouts, ratings, safety performance, or permission to energize. The package is not ready for fabrication, procurement release, wiring, energization, or operation around people.

## Corrections completed in V2.1

1. Synchronized the authoritative specification and BOM from PNOZ X2.8P to the preliminary PNOZ s4 750104 proposal.
2. Verified and documented the Pilz falling-edge monitored-start mode and the exact `S12 -> momentary NO reset -> K1 NC -> K2 NC -> S34` chain. No nonexistent `S33` terminal was added.

6. Prohibited ordinary fully populated DYNAMIXEL 3-pin TTL and 4-pin RS-485 daisy cables in the individually protected star-injection topology until a released VDD-isolating harness passes continuity and no-backfeed tests.
7. Added U2D2 interfaces `UV1`, `UA1`, and `UB1` to the connector schedule and added IDEC and U2D2 Power Hub manufacturer records.
8. Replaced premature fuse/conductor language with Mean Well overload, hiccup, peak-load, and inrush evidence plus the unpublished inputs required for coordination.
9. Added the LRS-350 115/230 V selector inspection gate, output-terminal allocation/current-sharing inputs, rapid-cycle/regeneration inputs, and protection-selectivity inputs.
10. Reflowed sheet 06 after visual review found overlapping labels in an earlier V2.1 iteration. The final export has no observed clipping or overlap.

## Prioritized residual issues

### BLOCKER

1. **Exact E-stop devices and physical terminal views are not selected.**  
   **Location:** sheet 02 `S0`, terminals `TBD-C1A/C1B/C2A/C2B`, nets `V0_ESTOP_CH1_FEED`, `V0_ESTOP_CH1_RETURN`, `V0_ESTOP_CH2_FEED`, `V0_ESTOP_CH2_RETURN`; sheet 06 `SH0` and corresponding `HR30_ESTOP_*` nets.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** freeze the complete IDEC order code, reset action, operator, color/legend, contact blocks, cover/IP20 treatment, environmental and approval applicability, contact rating/minimum load, panel/cable/strain-relief details, and exact bottom-view drawing. Continuity-test the received device before replacing any `TBD-*` identifier.

2. **Reset-to-motion exclusion is not physically or programmatically proven.**  
   **Location:** sheet 02 `S1/SR1`; sheet 04 `RT1`, nets `V0_SAFE_READY_LOGIC`, `SOFTWARE_ARM_REQUEST`, `TORQUE_ENABLE_REQUEST`, `MOTION_TARGET_REQUEST`; sheet 06 `SH1/SRH1` and `HR30_*` equivalents.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** release the controller state machine, initialize goals to a documented non-motion state, require a distinct deliberate ARM transition, and pass power-up, E-stop release, reset, stale-goal, nonzero-goal, watchdog, and fault-injection tests. A connected reset net is not evidence that motion cannot occur.

3. **Redundant actuator-power interruption cannot be rated or credited.**  
   **Location:** sheet 02 `K1`, `K2`, `FSR1`, `SK1`, `SK2`, nets `V0_12V_FUSED`, `V0_K1_LOAD`, `V0_12V_SAFE`, `V0_K1_COIL_CMD`, `V0_K2_COIL_CMD`; sheet 06 `KH1`, `KH2`, `FSRH1`, `SKH1`, `SKH2`, and corresponding `HR30_*` nets.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** select exact contactors with applicable DC making/breaking, capacitive inrush, mirror-contact and minimum-load evidence; select output protection and suppression; calculate fault coordination; measure dropout/stopping time; perform welded-contact and single-fault tests. The Pilz maximum protection values are not selections.

4. **Mains, protection, conductors, and enclosure remain non-buildable.**  
   **Location:** sheet 01 `JAC1`, `Q0`, `QF1-QF3`, `PS1-PS3`, `PETB1`, `SP1`; sheet 03 `F0-F3`; sheet 14 `TBAC1`, `TB24`, `TBV0`, `TBHR30`.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** prospective AC/DC fault current, jurisdiction, installation category, cable lengths, ambient, bundling, insulation, connector/terminal limits, selector position control, enclosure thermal result, current sharing, supply inrush maximum/T50/waveform/I-squared-t, hiccup waveform/restart duty, downstream capacitance, actuator regeneration, and selected protection curves/interrupt ratings.

5. **The HR-30 battery/BMS/precharge/charging system is topology only.**  
   **Location:** sheet 05 `BATH1`, `BMSH1`, `FBAT1`, `SDH1`, `KPH1`, `RPH1`, `KBH1`, `JCHG1`, `TLMH1`, and all `PACK_*`, `PRECHARGE_*`, `CHARGE_*` nets.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** qualified pack supplier interface, chemistry/voltage window, pack fault current and impedance, BMS port behavior, fuse and DC interrupt rating, service disconnect, contactors, resistor/energy/timing, voltage threshold, discharge path, charger/interlock, telemetry isolation, enclosure and thermal design, and restrained fault tests.

6. **Individually protected DYNAMIXEL branches are not proven isolated in the physical harness.**  
   **Location:** sheet 03 `M1-M3`, `UV1:2`; sheets 07-08 `ML1-ML6`, `MR1-MR6`; sheets 09-10 `M31-M35`, `M41-M45`, `M51-M53`, `UA1:2`, `UB1:2`; sheet 14 `JHL1`, `JHR1`, `JUA1`, `JUB1`.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** released harness drawings that omit or isolate VDD on every data-only link, physical connector-view evidence, per-end continuity records, no-backfeed tests, and proof that branch outputs cannot be paralleled through an actuator, cable, U2D2, or Power Hub.

### MAJOR

1. **The PNOZ s4 physical mode and start/feedback routing remain unverified.**  
   **Location:** sheet 02 `SR1/S1/K1/K2`; sheet 06 `SRH1/SH1/KH1/KH2`; `S12-reset-EDM-S34` nets.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** with supply removed, set and seal the manual page-13 lower-row/third-column mode; record received-device identity and terminal marking; route the start/feedback loop separately or protect it; test stuck reset, induced short, intermittent bridge, feedback contact, timing, and unexpected restart. `41-42` and `Y32` remain diagnostic only.

   **Location:** `UV1:2`, `UA1:2`, `UB1:2`, function `TTL_VDD`, currently no-connect in the proposed custom cable.

3. **RS-485 physical-layer selections remain open.**  
   **Location:** sheets 07-08 `XCVRL1`, `XCVRR1`, `RTL1/RTL2`, `RTR1/RTR2`, `RS485_LEFT_*`, `RS485_RIGHT_*`; sheet 14 `JHL1/JHR1`.

4. **PE bonding and the proposed DC 0 V/PE star point are intent only.**  
   **Location:** sheet 01 `PETB1`, `SP1`, net `PE`; sheet 14 `SHLD1`, `TP1`.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** panel/layout drawing, bonding hardware and conductor selection, inspection points, EMC/fault-current review, unintended-bond survey, PE continuity test, and jurisdiction-applicable insulation/dielectric tests. `SP1` remains open by default.

5. **Sensor sheets are connected functional interfaces, not production circuits.**  
   **Location:** sheets 11-13, `LCL1-LCL4`, `LCR1-LCR4`, `ADCL1`, `ADCR1`, `IMU1-IMU3`, `AFE1`.

6. **The logical wire table is not a physical conductor/core schedule.**  
   **Location:** `wire-number-table.csv`, especially multi-drop power, return, bus, and PE nets.  
   **Correction/evidence required:** a released conductor schedule assigning every physical core, segment, origin/destination terminal, color, gauge, insulation, length, route, shield/drain, ferrule/contact, and test record.
