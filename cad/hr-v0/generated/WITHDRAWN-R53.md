# R53 arm-geometry withdrawal

**DO NOT QUOTE OR FABRICATE MV0-001, MV0-002, OR MV0-003 FROM THIS DIRECTORY.**

The R0.1/P0.2 generated files are retained as historical space-model evidence. Exact ROBOTIS STEP coordinates show that their flat-plate arm arrangement does not define the required H101 moving-frame to S101/S102 body-frame transforms. The active mechanical boundary is `HR-V0-MECH-P0.3`; its J1/J2/G1/OMAX datums are blank and its supplier-packet set contains zero ZIPs.

Only `cad/hr-v0/generated/vendor-interfaces/` is current R53 geometric evidence. It demonstrates the orientation defect; it is not a replacement arm design.
