What this changes

The derived ratio is a sizing direction, not a released transmission. It must be reconciled with packaging, belt capacity, backlash, reflected inertia, output speed, motor torque-speed behavior, efficiency, electrical current and heat. The contact-enabled inverse is also specific to MuJoCo's soft-contact and ideal-fixture assumptions.
