Contact rules that prevent an accidental power path

  • STDC14 pin 3 senses CTRL_3V3. It does not power the target.
  • STDC14 pins 5, 7 and 11 are target signal ground, including GNDDETECT.
  • Pins 1, 2, 8, 9, 10, 13 and 14 are explicit no-connects.
