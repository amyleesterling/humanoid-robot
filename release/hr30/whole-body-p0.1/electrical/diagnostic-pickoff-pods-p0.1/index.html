No three-metre cable is directly attached to a robot power or safety node. Each long conductor begins after two source-local resistors.

What it cannot fix

The short conductor from each source terminal to JIN is upstream of the resistors. Exact terminal hardware, route and retention must be accepted before connection.

No safety function

The pods are diagnostic test equipment. They do not perform emergency stopping, power interruption or fault-tolerant monitoring.
