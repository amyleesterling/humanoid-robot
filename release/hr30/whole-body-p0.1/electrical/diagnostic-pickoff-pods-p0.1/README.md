# HR-30 source-local diagnostic pickoff pods P0.1

**PRELIMINARY - UNBUILT SOURCE-LOCAL DIAGNOSTIC PICKOFF PODS - ZERO SAFETY CREDIT - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, CONNECTION, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, WALKING OR ENERGIZATION**

Eight identical one-channel pods place two 100 kOhm Vishay TNPW1206 resistors in each HI and LO lead before any three-metre cable reaches the floating measurement panel. Each exact HR-30 signal gets its own 74 x 34 mm native KiCad board in a Hammond 1551KFLBK envelope candidate. No signal reference, shield, enclosure or channel is shared.

The complete nominal chain is 200 kOhm per pod lead, 10.2 kOhm per measurement-panel lead and the nominal 1 MOhm NI-9229 differential input. Its paper ratio is 0.704027035 and scale correction is 1.420400; this is not calibration evidence. The source terminal to first resistor remains upstream of protection. Exact device-specific tap accessories, <=100 mm guarded tails, loading/fault review, PCB/enclosure FAI, build, calibration and FER-G11 remain open.
