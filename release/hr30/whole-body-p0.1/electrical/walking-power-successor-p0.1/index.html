Why two devices are required

TI documents that TPS25948 can pass bidirectional steady-state current when RCBCTRL is low, but its overcurrent protection acts only from IN to OUT. The selected P0.1 candidate therefore places one device in each direction. This remains an engineering inference requiring TI application review and physical validation.

Why the topology changed

The earlier five-instance grouping was rejected during whole-body reconciliation. A shared-input arm board would tie RS-LARM to RS-RARM. A shared-input distal board would tie TTL-LDIST, TTL-RDIST and TTL-HEAD together downstream of three independent 9 V regulators.

The corrected package instantiates one six-channel pattern for each of the eight authoritative buses. Empty positions are DNP and do not create another feed.

  • WPS-H02 current-limit setpoints, resistors and ITIMER values are not selected
  • WPS-H03 six XC330 caps are 0.700 A, below the TPS25948 1 A minimum limit
  • WPS-H04 datasheet maximum current-limit accuracy is stated only above 3 A while every HR-30 candidate cap is below 3 A
  • WPS-H05 eight bus-domain brake/dump circuits have no selected devices, thresholds or energy ratings
  • WPS-H06 source absorption and reverse-energy behavior are not characterized
  • WPS-H07 paired startup, disable and fault timing are unvalidated
  • WPS-H08 native schematic is not a routed PCB
  • WPS-H09 connector, conductor, fuse and eight-feed coordination remain open
  • WPS-H10 dynamic walking demand and regenerative events are unmeasured
  • WPS-H11 functional safety allocation and stopping performance are not validated
  • WPS-H12 no procurement, fabrication, connection, powered-test, motion or energization authority exists
