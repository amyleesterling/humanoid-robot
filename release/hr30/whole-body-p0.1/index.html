The dimensions come from the specification

What this pass proves—and what it does not

Native geometry exists

STEP and GLB are generated from a versioned CadQuery source. The STEP reimports with vertices exactly at Z=0 and Z=762 mm.

Kinematic architecture exists

All 25 candidate axes have coordinates, directions, regions, and provisional ranges in a machine-readable schedule.

Preferred reach is missed

The specified nominal segments total 370 mm per arm and 950 mm span. These pass the 390/980 mm hard limits but miss the 360/900 mm targets.

P0.1 mass envelope closes only in the planning model

The explicit model is 11.283 kg including 0.554 kg of located joint-fastener candidates, and 11.652 kg after the 0.369 kg unused integration reserve. It has 0.348 kg to the 12 kg P0.1 maximum, but misses the 10 kg lightweight stretch objective by 1.652 kg. Received-part variation is not closed.
