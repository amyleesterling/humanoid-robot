The non-negotiable boundary

JST publishes 3 A for EH at AWG 22 under its stated conditions. The 0.848 A arithmetic distance from the raw-800 internal screen is not measurement uncertainty, transient margin or thermal proof. Reaching 3 A is a test-abort ceiling until a stricter pre-trigger and complete acceptance basis are approved.
