Continuous clearance, not a sampling trick

The 1° samples are useful for audit and visualization, but they do not prove what happens between poses. A separate adaptive calculation covers all 69 nonintentional solid-pair groups over J1 −20°…70° and J2 15°…115°. It accepted 130 interval cells and used 85 exact B-Rep distance calls where bounding-box evidence was insufficient.
