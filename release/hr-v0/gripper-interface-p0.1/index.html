Safety boundary

The Maestro, regulator PG and feedback channel receive zero functional-safety credit. They improve ordinary monitoring only. The dual-channel E-stop/contactors remain the physical power-removal boundary, and the complete safety function still requires the project safety-requirements specification, PLr/SIL allocation, common-cause review, stopping-time limits and qualified validation.
