The source boundary

Manufacturer statement

The instruction applies torque M to gland installation with a locknut and to the cap nut. For M12, M is 1.5 N·m.

What stays open

The VDE table leaves separate locknut torque blank. No reviewed record provides the through-hole diameter. Exact cable/sheath behavior and completed-enclosure rating require evidence.
