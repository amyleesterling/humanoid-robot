The shortest honest path to a usable site

1

Name the exact premises

Facility, street address, room, bench, responsible representative and written permission.

2

Survey power and the bench

Receptacle, branch, ground, polarity, GFCI, fault-current basis, cord path, bench structure and restraint.

3

Set people and emergency controls

Exclusion boundary, adult supervision, child lockout, emergency contacts, shutdown and fire-response rules.

4

Obtain qualified dispositions

Code/permit applicability, electrical review and a configuration-specific written E2 authorization with stop conditions.
