What actually changed

The P0.9 limiting feature was the unused upper edge of the moving adapter plate. P1.0 lowers that central edge from Z=+20 to +15 mm, retains a local M5 boss to Z=+17 mm, and keeps the original external stop surface beginning at Z=+19.9167 mm. The external lobes remain integral 2.5-D geometry and the hole axes do not move.
