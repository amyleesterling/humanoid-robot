Decision

A stationary reaction-torque sensor is the preferred evidence route because it can observe torque throughout a motion profile without promoting motor current into torque evidence. FUTEK TFF400 item FSH04015 is an exact evaluation candidate, not selected hardware. The custom fixed/active adapters, structural joins, guard, catch, anchor, load device and every powered limit remain unresolved.
