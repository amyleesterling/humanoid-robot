Why this is safer to evaluate

The probe jaw surrounds one insulated conductor. It does not add a resistor, capacitance or galvanic return to the monitored node. That removes the specific divider-loading mechanism, but it does not prove noninterference: jaw fit, conductor movement, magnetic influence, calibration, polarity, thresholds and synchronized timing still require physical evidence.

Twelve closure holds

  1. AS-BUILT CONDUCTOR IDENTITY: Received terminal identities, from/to schedule, wire part, gauge, insulation OD, length and photographs must bind each W-number to one physical conductor
  2. JAW FIT AND ROUTING: A 5 mm-jaw clearance study must prove one-conductor capture without pinching, moving, opening or magnetically coupling adjacent conductors
  3. HOST SELECTION: Exact compatible TekVPI oscilloscope model/options, channel count, sample rate, record length, trigger and export path are required
  4. CALIBRATION: Current probe and host require in-date traceable calibration, degauss/zero records and an end-to-end injected-current check
  5. POLARITY AND THRESHOLDS: Every conductor direction, current sign, pulse/steady threshold, hysteresis, dwell and invalid-data rule remains SELECTION REQUIRED
  6. NONINTERFERENCE: Repeated jaw-open versus jaw-closed disconnected-load E2 sequences must show no state, timing, reset, EDM or dropout change within accepted limits
  7. SIMULTANEITY: Probe quantity and shared-timebase allocation must cover each claimed causal timing measurement; sequential runs cannot prove simultaneity
  8. MOTION WITNESS: An independent calibrated angle/displacement channel must prove stopping and no reset/ARM-commanded motion
  9. SOURCE WITNESS: An accepted isolated control-rail voltage channel must separate command-state changes from source collapse or brownout
  10. E2 BOUNDARY: Actuator source absent; K1/K2 load poles unsourced and unwired; guarded access, qualified authorization and abort conditions required
  11. UNCERTAINTY: Probe accuracy/delay, host timing, thresholds, motion witness, source witness, alignment and repeatability must close the R174 budget
  12. QUALIFIED REVIEW: Qualified electrical and functional-safety reviewers must accept the exact setup, failure analysis, results and limitations
