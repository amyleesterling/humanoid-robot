Mass-screen effect

Replacing the 82 g XM430 line with the 23 g XC330 reduces the incomplete known/CAD subtotal from 692.758 g to 633.758 g. Adding this branch's full-density custom parts gives 673.775 g and leaves 76.225 g for every still-unmeasured frame, screw, cable, strain relief, bumper and integration item. That is progress, but not closure.

Decision

This is the preferred lightweight feasibility branch, not the selected production gripper. It may replace the RM-X52/XM430 path only after an approved change, complete frame/interface CAD, exact hardware, tolerance and print records, received metrology, guarded force/drop/wear testing, mass closure, and qualified review.
