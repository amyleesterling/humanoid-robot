Decision

The preferred first support inquiry is an 80/20 40200-SP-K static pedestal configured at 300 mm with a modified 40006-BP blank plate. The manufacturer's 2,040 N·m figure is relevant only when floor-mounted. It is not a project allowable and does not validate Boston's unknown substrate or anchors.

Inspect the vertical-axis candidate

The gold floor plane and thin centerline are datums, not pedestal geometry. Exact pedestal CAD is deliberately absent pending the manufacturer response.
