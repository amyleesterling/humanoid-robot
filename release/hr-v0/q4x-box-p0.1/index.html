Protection arithmetic without wishful thinking

33.125 mA is a steady-state catalog screen, not an inrush result. The 3.019 ratio to the 0.1 A breaker is not a safety factor. Phoenix's 1.2× current-limiting figure is typical, so R184 does not claim a hard 0.12 A ceiling.

Drain and ground treatment

The cordset drain parks on insulated terminal XQ1.6. There is no bridge, shield clamp, PE, rail, chassis or 0 V connection. Q4X analog ground pin 5 is not project-bonded to DC common pin 3. Received noise and isolation tests can reject this proposal.
