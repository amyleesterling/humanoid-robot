Who could machine the five HR‑V0 aluminum parts?

PRELIMINARY — NOT APPROVED FOR QUOTATION, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION.

Decision at a glance

Kontrast4D has the strongest local published evidence. Protolabs and Xometry are the strongest online review routes. No provider is qualified, contacted, quoted, or authorized.

Material stays exact: 6061‑T651. A page that says only 6061 or 6061‑T6 does not close the requirement.
Strongest local evidence

Kontrast4D

Salem, Massachusetts. Published multi-axis CNC, numerical tolerance ranges, CMM and FAI capability.

Still needs written T651, stock, full-drawing, countersink, rail-datum and surface-map acceptance.

Exact material published

Protolabs

Official material page lists 6061‑T651; milling pages describe FAI and certification options.

C06/C07 tight controls and the actual factory/network path remain unverified.

Alternate

Xometry

Published broad machining and inspection capability through a manufacturing network.

Exact provider, T651 stock and complete drawing acceptance remain unknown.

Material hold

Fictiv

Published CNC capability is promising, but the reviewed page identifies 6061‑T6.

T6 is not silently interchangeable with the controlled T651 requirement.

Evidence incomplete

Other New England shops

Accurate Tool and Machine, T&D Specialties, Tucker Engineering and United Precision Manufacturing remain research candidates.

Their public evidence does not close all material, numerical tolerance, CMM/FAI and operation requirements.

Excluded finished-part route

SendCutSend

Potential blank-source research only under a separate control.

Published T6 laser route and tolerances do not satisfy the finished T651 CNC part definition.

Inputs we still must choose

“Light duty” is the intent, not the calculation. Before drawing release we need numerical payload, motion profile, duty cycle, restraint/fall cases, safety factors, C05 joint proof and C06/C07 impact inputs.
