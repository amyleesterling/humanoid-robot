What blocks an actual harness

  1. CHU-001HAR-CIN source-side termination

    exact fuse holder/source terminal, approved contact/lug/ferrule, wire range, torque/tool and finger-safe enclosure route

  2. CHU-002carrier placement

    controlled mounting location, orientation, clearance, thermal airflow, tie-downs and service access

  3. CHU-003cut lengths

    measured route for HAR-CIN and HAR-COUT including service loop and bend limits

  4. CHU-004strip length and crimp geometry

    current JST application specification or qualified harness-provider process for SVH-21T-P1.1 with Belden 9918

  5. CHU-005manual/prototype crimp tool

    manufacturer-approved exact tool or qualified provider process; catalog machine chain alone is not a prototype hand-tool release

  6. CHU-006pull test and sampling

    applicable workmanship standard, contact/wire minimum, coupon quantity and calibrated tester

  7. CHU-007labels and strain relief

    exact materials, compatibility, placement, abrasion, heat and retention tests

  8. CHU-008installed electrical limits

    fault current, fuse, source foldback, length, ambient, bundling, connector, inrush, regeneration, duty and jurisdiction evidence

  9. CHU-009post-carrier net naming

    Electrical V3 and DXL-star revision that distinguishes BRANCH_LIMITED_OUT from the pre-carrier J1/J2/J3_VDD names
