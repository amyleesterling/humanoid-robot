Findings that remain open

  • MAJORMPAR-F01 C04/C05/C06/C07 readable drawings do not graphically dimension every controlled feature; six controls are schedule-bound rather than fully displayed.R135 binds each schedule row and adds interactive DXF maps; qualified reviewer must decide whether released supplier drawings need conventional dimensioned views.
  • MAJORMPAR-F02 C06/C07 DXFs intentionally contain pre-fillet construction profiles while STEP contains the R2 finished profile.Do not machine from DXF alone; provider must bind STEP plus drawing/control schedule and return its interpreted manufacturing model for review.
  • BLOCKERMPAR-F03 Nominal file parity cannot prove material, tolerance capability, received fit, stop load, strength, fatigue, impact, stopping or safety.Execute supplier DFM, FAI, received-interface metrology, structural/stop analysis, proof and qualified review before any fabrication or energization release.
  • MAJORMPAR-F04 C01/C04/C06/C07 STEP countersink openings are diameter 11.40 mm while DXF/drawing nominal is 11.30 mm with +0.10/-0.00 tolerance.Qualified review must decide whether to remodel STEP at nominal or explicitly declare upper-limit model semantics; supplier may not infer nominal or tolerance from STEP alone.
