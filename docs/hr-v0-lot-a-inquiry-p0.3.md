## Provider response boundary

Each metrology candidate receives a separate 33-question response template and separate 18-characteristic bid template. Each ROBOTIS route receives only its own eight sales/order or four technical questions. No candidate receives another provider's bid surface.

Providers are asked to:

1. acknowledge the exact attachment hashes;
2. match every received feature against the 79 source signatures;
3. report missing, extra, inaccessible or nonmatching features without substitution;
4. answer BID or NO BID for every method and characteristic;
5. identify equipment, calibration, performing facility and accredited scope;
6. disclose support, datum, fit, outlier, repetition and uncertainty methods; and
7. return raw data, transforms, residuals, processing logs and attributable evidence.

CAD nominals remain comparison aids only. They do not establish received conformity, tolerances, thread function, accepted datums, fit, assembly instructions or safety approval.
