# Verification Scope and Applicability

**PRELIMINARY—NOT APPROVED FOR FABRICATION OR ENERGIZATION**

This table prevents environmental and lifecycle topics from disappearing merely because no hardware exists yet. “Selection required” means the applicable standard, severity, equipment, sample size, and pass/fail threshold must be derived from the released intended use, jurisdiction, design, and risk assessment. No row is evidence of compliance.

| Category | HR-V0 | HR-30A/B | HR-30C/D/W | Required evidence before release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EMC emissions and immunity | Selection required | Selection required | Selection required | Applicable emissions/immunity standard; cable and enclosure configuration; conducted/radiated, ESD, EFT/surge and RF tests; safe fault response |
| Ingress and contamination | Dry indoor baseline; rating selection required | Selection required | Floor dust, debris and accidental liquid exposure review required | Enclosure/connector IP target or documented exclusion; probe/dust/liquid test; cleaning boundary |
| Transport and storage | Selection required | Selection required | Battery transport adds separate requirements | Shock, vibration, packaging, temperature/humidity storage, post-transport inspection and calibration retention |
| Maintenance and service | Selection required | Selection required | Selection required | Lockout/isolation, residual-energy discharge, lifting/restraint, tool access, replacement and post-service validation procedures |
| Foreseeable misuse and abuse | Required | Required | Required | Scenario register for climbing, pushing, snagging, blocked joints, dropped objects, wrong charger, wrong connector and unauthorized reset; justified exclusions and tests |
| Fatigue and endurance | 100 handoffs is only functional acceptance | Cycle targets required | Gait, joint, belt, harness and cover life targets required | Load spectra, sample size, inspection intervals, wear limits and end-of-test teardown |
| Thermal cycling | Selection required | Selection required | Battery and converter cycling required | Released hot/cold dwell and powered-cycle profile; condensation exclusion; post-cycle functional and insulation checks |
| Connector and harness retention | Required | Required | Flexing leg/tether duty required | Exact connector/contact MPNs; mating cycles; pull/retention; bend/flex; fretting; continuity; temperature rise; no-backfeed tests |
| Battery/charger abuse | Not applicable while V0 is mains/tether only | Not applicable to battery-free HR-30A; reassess later phases | Required for HR-30W | Qualified pack evidence plus overcharge, short, charger mismatch, thermal propagation/containment and transport review under applicable rules |

The procedure registry defines controlled IDs used by current requirements and release documents. Category-specific procedures shall be added once applicability and limits are selected. Until then, these rows remain release blockers rather than implicit passes.
