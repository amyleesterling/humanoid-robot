# HR-V0 U2D2-to-JC1 manufacturer-build request P0.1

> **PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, CONNECTION, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

`HR-V0-U2D2-JC1-HARNESS-RFQ-P0.1` is a deterministic, **UNSENT** technical and commercial request for the R261 controller harness. It identifies JST Guntersville Assembly Manufacturing as the primary evidence-acquisition route because JST publicly describes that operation as a full-service custom cable/harness assembler.

JST/GAM already publishes standard double-ended EH leads using `SEH-001T-P0.6`, AWG 22 UL1007 black wire and controlled applicator processing. Its current catalog stops at 304.8 mm inside length. Those standard single leads are not the required 500 mm, two-color, two-conductor assembly and are explicitly rejected as the complete harness. No splice is permitted.

The unsent package requests:

- one first article and a separate quote for five evaluation articles;
- `EHR-3` at both ends with `SEH-001T-P0.6` only in cavities 1 and 3;
- physically empty cavity 2 at both ends;
- A1-to-B1 GND and A3-to-B3 DATA with no other path and no splice;
- requested Belden `3051 BK005` black and `3051 WH005` white, or an exact documented substitute requiring acceptance;
- the project `500 +/- 5 mm` finished datum and `25 +/- 5 mm` pair-lay candidates;
- provider-numbered mating/rear views that eliminate visual-orientation inference;
- controlled process, first-article, continuity, isolation, population, traceability and conformity evidence; and
- MOQ, charges, price, lead time, quote validity and Boston shipping terms.

The payload does not ask the project to infer proprietary crimp dimensions. It asks the manufacturer for numeric limits where releasable or an attributable conformity statement tied to its controlled JST process, tool/applicator identity and validation basis. Qualified review and first-article evidence remain mandatory.
