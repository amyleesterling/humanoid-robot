# HR-V0 J2 soft-contact pad P0.1

> **PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, QUOTATION, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, CONNECTION, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

R275 names Rogers `2300327` / PORON `4790-92-25024-04P` as the preferred unselected test coupon. Its published 0.61 +/-0.08 mm thickness fits the current 0.75 mm envelope, but its compression data are not an energy- or stall-stop rating.

The current unaccepted inertia gives 0.000154502 J at 10 deg/s. By contrast, the radius-basis endpoint force doing work through 0.75 mm gives 0.142290750 J, about 921.0 times the kinetic energy. That comparison is deliberately an order-of-magnitude warning: exact contact-normal work and measured torque decay remain open. It proves why the metal backup must remain structural and why power-removal time dominates.

ACE `MC5M-3-B`, the smallest catalogued energy-rated comparator found, needs 4 mm stroke and a minimum listed impact speed about 59 times HR-V0's nominal 10 deg/s tangent speed; it is rejected for the current envelope.
