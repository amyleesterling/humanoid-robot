## Controlled baseline summary

This baseline contains 80 draft requirements and 40 open risks. HR-V0 and HR-30 release specifications remain unbuilt and unvalidated. Individual files may advance only through a recorded change that updates their revision, linked requirements, evidence, and review disposition as applicable. `HR-V0-RC-P0.1` provides deterministic package membership and hashes; `EG-002` remains partial until the candidate is merged or otherwise immutably accepted and qualified reviewers sign the applicable stage. `HR-V0-BOM-P0.1` advances `EG-003` to partial but does not release a complete machine BOM. `HR-V0-MECH-P0.3` preserves base coordination while explicitly withdrawing the invalid arm chain; `EG-005` through `EG-008` remain partial pending replacement geometry, physical evidence and qualified review. `HR-V0-E2-SEQ-P0.1` supplies commissioning inputs only; every E2 gate remains unresolved pending execution and qualified authorization.

## Release rule

Neither a clean ERC result nor a website deployment constitutes design approval. The package remains **PRELIMINARY—NOT APPROVED FOR ENERGIZATION** until the applicable selections, calculations, physical tests, and qualified electrical and functional-safety reviews are complete.
