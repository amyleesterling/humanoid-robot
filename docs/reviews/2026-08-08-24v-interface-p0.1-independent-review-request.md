**PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR FABRICATION OR ENERGIZATION**

Please review accuracy and completeness, not presentation polish or approval status.

Scope: Electrical `V3-P1.10`, `HR-V0-24V-IF-P0.1`, `HR-V0-CP-P0.4` and `HR-V0-E2-HW-P0.1`.

## Questions

1. Does current official Mean Well evidence support using `DC PLUG-P1J-R7B` with `GST40A24-P1J`? If not, identify the exact written evidence required.
2. Is the adapter cable's current/application envelope documented anywhere in current official Mean Well material?
3. Independently reconcile the Mean Well R7B plug assignment with the Kycon `KPJX-PM-4S` jack-view drawing. Identify any view/orientation ambiguity.
4. Is `J24:1/4 -> SAFETY_24V_RAW` and `J24:2/3 -> SAFETY_0V` a faithful topology model without implying current sharing or redundancy?
5. Is `F24` correctly separated from the connector, and are all inputs required to select its hardware/value identified?
6. Does the panel package avoid releasing a cutout, PCB/harness, wire, retention, ingress or strain-relief design?
7. Does the E2 package keep the 12 V actuator source and branches physically absent/disconnected and all powered work unauthorized?
8. Re-run KiCad ERC and all four relevant checkers. Report any inconsistent reference, terminal, net, BOM, panel, E2, export or source-register record.
9. Identify any missing fault case: open/shorted/incorrectly keyed adapter, reversed pin view, single contact open, unequal current division, intermittent retention, contact heating, source brownout or branch-protection failure.
10. State what remains necessary before procurement, fabrication, wiring, connection and controlled first energization. Do not mark the package approved.

Primary evidence must be current official manufacturer documentation with revision/date recorded. Do not infer compatibility, current rating, fuse value, wire size, cutout or order code.
