# Sol R12 status after R109

Status: **PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

R109 closes one narrow source-control subfinding within Sol R12's missing-buildable-mechanical-definition blocker. Six current manufacturer payloads now define reference geometry for the two FR12-G101GM frame-set parts, FR12-E170 and FR12-E171, and the real binaries are controlled by source endpoint, date, size, SHA-256 hash and signature.

Sol's blocker remains open. The drawings are explicitly `FOR REFERENCE ONLY`; material, tolerances, drawing revision, complete mechanism geometry, assembly mates, H104 registration, installed mass/COM/inertia, guard, force/current, wear, retention, drop evidence and qualified acceptance remain unresolved. The source correction closes no energization or fabrication gate. HR-V0 remains not ready to fabricate, assemble, connect or energize; HR-30W remains a staged feasibility program rather than a demonstrated walking machine.
