# Sol R12 status after R208

**PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, CONNECTION, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

This is a project-owned reconciliation against Sol's existing R12 review, not a new Sol review and not an approval.

R208 advances the architecture-only compute/observation finding by proving the intended one-source 3V3 topology, enumerating partial-power states and faults, and recording exact current primary-source gaps. It does not close a Sol finding.

R208 also exposes a new circuit blocker: the current 1.00 kohm ISO1212 output-series candidate does not bound a nominal 3.3 V hard short within TI's 3 mA recommended output-current envelope. Raspberry Pi 5 header-load capability, RP1 DC input limits, a corrected RSO strategy, signal margin, back-power/ramp behavior, physical evidence and qualified review remain open.

Sol's R12 totals and overall verdict therefore remain unchanged. No energization gate or work authority closes.
