# Sol R12 status after R155

R155 narrows one portion of Sol's external branch-current and regenerative-energy blocker. It supplies a connected, source-backed candidate circuit and executable evidence route. It does not close the blocker.

Still open are the physical external-current envelope, reverse-current magnitude, shunt pulse energy and failure behavior, PCB and harness definition, source/contactor/fuse coordination, simultaneous-axis regeneration, thermal and EMC evidence, HIL/fault injection, qualified review and work authorization. Sol's verdict that HR-V0 is not ready to build or energize therefore remains controlling.
