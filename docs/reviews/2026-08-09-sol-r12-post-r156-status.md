# Sol R12 status after R156

R156 is a project-owned response to Sol's missing buildable electrical/physical-evidence finding. It is not a new Sol review and does not change the original `18 BLOCKER / 30 MAJOR / 8 MINOR` totals.

R156 narrows one concrete gap: the retained forward-current-limiter candidate now has exact source-backed component identities, five native KiCad sheets, a routed four-layer single-channel carrier, a drawing-derived RPW footprint, controlled three-article variants, review CAM outputs and blank physical test records. The initial visually crowded schematic export was rejected and split into legible core, threshold, transient and measurement sheets.

Sol's blocker remains open. Independent footprint/DFM acceptance, physical articles, forward/reverse current waveforms, regenerative pulse energy, source/contactor/fuse coordination, connector/harness limits, thermal/EMC/HIL/fault evidence, grounding, qualified review and work authorization remain absent. HR-V0 is not ready to build or energize, and HR-30W remains a later feasibility program.
