# Sol R12 findings rechecked against R74

**PRELIMINARY—NOT APPROVED FOR FABRICATION, MOTION, CONNECTION, OR ENERGIZATION.**

This is a project-owned status reconciliation, not a new Sol review and not an approval. Sol's R12 totals remain **18 BLOCKER / 30 MAJOR / 8 MINOR** against the pre-correction baseline.

## What changed

R74 addresses part of Sol's guard-definition finding. `HR-V0-GUARD-P0.2` now provides a controlled coordinate system, 400 × 900 × 950 mm internal clear-space candidate, source STEP/GLB, sixteen frame-length candidates, eight panel-envelope candidates, a catch receiver, an interactive guide and twelve current P0.7 inspection cases. It also records the 0.931632 J maximum 100 g foam drop-energy screen as a test input only.

The old statement that no panel thickness, span or guard geometry is specified is therefore stale for design-candidate geometry. It remains correct for a released guard because the nominal 6 mm panel is not an exact manufacturer grade, the 20 × 20 mm profile is not an exact verified product, and retention, joints, anchors, access, impact, stopping, sweep and physical validation are unresolved.

## Findings that remain materially open

- no buildable released mechanical definition or fabrication authority;
- no accepted complete gripper/payload/cable swept and stopping envelope;
- no guard safety-distance determination or selected access probe;
- no exact panel/frame/joint/fastener/anchor/cable-entry selections;
- no structural, impact, stability, detached-part or retention qualification;
- no executed guard, drop, stopping, access or fault evidence;
- no qualified mechanical, electrical or functional-safety acceptance;
- no HR-V0 energization authorization and no HR-30W walking demonstration.

R74 therefore changes evidence maturity, not the project verdict. HR-V0 remains not ready for fabrication or energization, and HR-30W remains physically plausible but unproven.
