# R252 independent mechanical and metrology review request

> **PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, CONNECTION, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

Please review `HR-V0-JOINT-STACK-FIXTURE-P0.1` and `HR-V0-CONFIG-REC-P0.16` as an independent design-evidence review. This is not a request to approve fabrication or conduct a shop session.

## Review questions

1. Do the controlled XM540, FR13-H101K, and FR13-S102K transforms reproduce the intended nominal joint stack?
2. Are the six proposed S102-face contact locations geometrically appropriate, and are any contacts, load paths, or keepouts missing?
3. Does the nominal `6.750 mm` minimum contact-to-XM540 screen support only the limited claim made, with received tolerances and uncertainty still open?
4. Can the proposed frame topology restrain the fixed S102 face without loading the actuator case, connector, H101, bends, holes, or candidate hard-stop regions?
5. What material, compliant contact, maximum preload, friction, secondary restraint, fastener, tolerance, and datum selections are required before a buildable fixture can exist?
6. Does the temporary sequence fail closed before screw insertion, washer/index/spacer placement, torque, locking, reuse, hand-positioned articulation, and teardown?
7. What exact manufacturer evidence is required to select screw length, thread engagement, temporary torque, tool, locking prohibition, and reuse/disposition?
8. Are the proposed first-article inspection, repeatability, contact-force, fit/keepout, uncertainty, evidence, and nonconformance controls sufficient for a zero-energy metrology fixture?
9. Can any fixture element become an unintended rotation stop, trap, pinch point, or source of hidden article damage?
10. What additional evidence is required before a qualified reviewer could authorize one configuration-bound, unpowered session?

## Required response format

For each finding, provide severity, exact artifact/row/feature, factual basis, required correction, evidence needed for closure, reviewer role/competence, and whether the issue blocks fixture fabrication, temporary assembly, the unpowered session, or all three. Do not grant functional-safety, powered-test, motion, or energization credit.
