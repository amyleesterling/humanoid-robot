# HR-V0 datum and GD&T qualified-review proposal P0.1

> **PRELIMINARY - NOT APPROVED FOR PROCUREMENT, FABRICATION, ASSEMBLY, CONNECTION, POWERED TESTING, MOTION, OR ENERGIZATION**

Identifier `HR-V0-GDT-REVIEW-P0.1`; round R250; formal GD&T is not released.

R250 proposes functional A/B/C datum schemes for the five current custom parts, twenty review-only feature controls, fifteen inspection-uncertainty allocations and a twelve-question qualified-review checklist. Nominal STEP/DXF/drawing geometry is unchanged.

The proposal uses [ASME Y14.5-2018 (R2024)](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-5-dimensioning-tolerancing/2018) as the product-definition basis. ASME states that Y14.5 establishes the symbols, rules, definitions and requirements for GD&T. Because Y14.5 is not a gaging standard, the inspection route separately references [ASME Y14.43-2011 (R2020)](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/y14-43-dimensioning-tolerancing-principles-gages-fixtures). Measurement uncertainty must be evaluated and reported under [NIST Technical Note 1297](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297).

The proposed 0.14 mm diametrical position is explicitly not released. It is a review candidate derived from the earlier ±0.05 mm rectangular coordinate zone; a qualified reviewer must determine functional equivalence, modifiers, datum precedence, contact/profile controls, inspection method and uncertainty before successor drawings can be issued.

Twelve holds and ten acceptances remain open. Provider DFM, first articles, received fit, qualified signature and separate fabrication/assembly authority remain absent.
